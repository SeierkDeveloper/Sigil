Pair with your vault

In your Sigil vault, open Apps, connect a Browser extension with "May suggest memories" turned on, and paste its app key here. What you save is encrypted in this browser for your vault and waits in its Inbox until you approve it.

The app key is kept in this browser's extension storage. Anyone with access to this browser profile could use it to suggest memories, never to read them unless you gave it folders. Revoke it in your vault at any time.
